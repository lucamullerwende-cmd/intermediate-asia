About Intermediate Asia

Commercial authority and entrepreneurial agility, headquartered in Hong Kong with affiliated partners across the region.

Empowering Businesses

Intermediate Asia is dedicated to providing tailored business affairs advice and development strategies to help emerging and established entities navigate complex landscapes and achieve sustainable growth, primarily, but not exclusively, in the Asia Pacific region. Our expert team across the AP region, headquartered in Hong Kong and with international and intercontinental affiliated partners, leverages years of experience to optimise operations and expand market reach.

The Founder provides a rare synthesis of commercial authority and entrepreneurial agility: with a 25-year plus track record in the Hong Kong and Asian markets, Gerald has served as a CEO, Executive Director, and Vice Chairman for firms ranging from TMT and startups to London plc’s.

Group expertise lies in navigating the complexities of strategic planning, business development, and corporate affairs across high-growth industries like TMT, renewables, MediTech, logistics, and asset-backed tokenisation. We specialise in supporting disruptive technologies and impact enterprises, ensuring that their global structuring and contractual foundations are as innovative as the solutions they provide.

Our Purpose

To empower businesses with expert advice, strategic partnerships, and investor introductions, guiding them towards sustainable growth and success in a rapidly evolving business environment.
